# Scene Blocker

Block scenes on floor plans with your crew. A free web app: open the link, Add to Home Screen, and it works on iPhone, Android and laptop (offline too).

- **Import script:** PDF, Final Draft (.fdx), Fountain or plain text. Scenes and characters are found automatically.
- **Floor plans:** add a top-down image per location.
- **Blocking:** characters as coloured circles (A1, A2...) joined by dotted move lines; cameras (A cam A1 to A2, B cam B1 to B2) with the lens under them; a free red dotted 180 line; lights, grip, sound, VT Station and vehicles.
- **Setups:** Setup 1, 2, 3... per scene, each with its own marks.
- **Storyboard:** upload frames per setup.
- **Export:** a picture of the plan with a title block.
- **Crew live mode (optional):** with a crew code, everyone sees each other's changes, who is online, which scene they are on and their pointer.

Your scripts, plans and frames stay on your device (or in your own crew database). Nothing private is stored in this repository.
