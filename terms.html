7. Privacy (POPIA)

On your device: your projects, plans and blocking are saved in your browser on your device.

In crew mode the following is stored in a Google Firebase database (Google is the service provider) so the crew can work together: the production (scenes, plans, blocking, frames, notes), the name you type, your colour, which scene you are on, your pointer position while online, and chat messages.

Activity log and feedback: to improve the app and support crews, the app records each visit in crew mode (your typed name, start time, time spent, a short list of actions such as opening a scene or adding a mark, and your browser type) and any feedback you send. Only the developer can read these. They are not sold or shared for advertising.

The app has no adverts and no tracking cookies. Data may be stored on servers outside South Africa. Use a first name or role instead of your full name if you prefer. You may ask the developer to see, correct or delete your data; leaving a crew removes the crew data from your device but not from the shared crew database, which the crew's organiser controls.
